I am a first-year PhD candidate at the HKUST NLP Group, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, where I am advised by Professor Junxian He. I received my B.Eng. degree from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024, where I was also advised by Professor Junxian He during my undergraduate studies. My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning.

Research Interests
======
* LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning
* Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)
